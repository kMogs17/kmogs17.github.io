From January 2016 to May 2020, I was a software engineer on a highly configurable and resilient monolithic healthcare claims adjudication platform supporting claims, members, providers, networks, and contracts.

This was a modern web based application using MVC patterns, utilizing Java, MySQL, Javascript, CSS, and HTML.

The team for this project grew from a medium co-located team (~35 people), to a large co-located team (~60 people) that also coordinated closely with several other co-located teams (~250 people), and several contractors and employees in India (~10 people).

During my time on this project, I had several notable achievements:

- Developed and maintained claims adjudication systems supporting complex healthcare workflows
- Engineered an adjudication engine achieving a **95%+ auto-adjudication rate**
- Implemented workflow-driven backend business logic aligned with domain rules and processing pipelines
- Led development of **division of financial responsibility** and **delegation engines**
- Built and maintained RESTful APIs and UI components for internal and customer-facing systems
- Implemented logging, monitoring, and code quality practices using **Splunk**, **SonarQube**, and automated unit/integration testing (Java, Groovy)
- Performed production triage ensuring **99% uptime** and rapid issue resolution across multiple environments
- Contributed to test automation frameworks and testing standards
- Started as senior apps developer and promoted to **Software Engineer** due to impact and consistency
