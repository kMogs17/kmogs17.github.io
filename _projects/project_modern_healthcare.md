From January 2016 to May 2020, I was a software engineer on a highly configurable and resilient monolithic healthcare claims adjudication platform supporting claims, members, providers, networks, and contracts.

This was a modern web based application using MVC patterns, utilizing Java, MySQL, Javascript, CSS, and HTML.

During my time on this project we grew from a medium co-located team (~35 people), to a large co-located team (~60 people) that also coordinated closely with several other co-located teams (~250 people).
We also coordinated with contractors and employees in India.

While building this application, I had several notable achievements.
I developed and maintained claims adjudication systems supporting complex healthcare workflows.
I engineered an adjudication engine achieving a 95%+ auto-adjudication rate.
I implemented workflow-driven backend business logic aligned with domain rules and processing pipelines.
I led development of division of financial responsibility and delegation engines.
I built and maintained RESTful APIs and UI components for internal and customer-facing systems.
I implemented logging, monitoring, and code quality practices using Splunk, SonarQube, and automated unit/integration testing (Java, Groovy).
I performed production triage ensuring 99% uptime and rapid issue resolution across multiple environments.
I contributed to test automation frameworks and testing standards.
